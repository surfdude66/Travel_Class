Travel Safety

Stay Safe Out There

Regional safety tips every traveller should know before departure.

🌏

Southeast Asia

  • Watch for common tourist scams — overpriced tuk-tuks, gem scams in Bangkok, distraction theft
  • Drink only bottled or filtered water; avoid ice in street stalls
  • Carry minimal cash; use hotel safes for passports and spare cards
  • Register your travel with your home country's embassy app
  • Dress modestly when visiting temples (cover shoulders & knees)
🗼

East Asia

  • Japan & Taiwan sit on active fault lines — know your hotel's evacuation plan
  • South Korea and Japan are extremely safe; petty crime is very low
  • Hong Kong: stay clear of large gatherings or political demonstrations
  • Learn how to say "I need help" in the local language
  • Carry a printed hotel address card — addresses can be hard to relay
🌊

Ocean & Pacific

  • Maldives & Phuket: always swim in designated areas with lifeguards
  • Jellyfish, sea urchins, and coral are common hazards — wear reef shoes
  • Australia: UV index is extreme — SPF 50+ sunscreen is non-negotiable
  • Riptides: swim parallel to shore to escape, never fight the current
  • Check local marine weather before boat trips or water sports
🧳

General Travel Tips

  • Always buy comprehensive travel insurance before departure
  • Follow the 3-2-1 medication rule: 3x what you need, 2 locations in luggage, 1 in carry-on
  • Save offline maps of your destinations (Google Maps, Maps.me)
  • Share your itinerary with someone at home who can raise alarm if needed
  • Keep digital copies of all documents in a cloud drive you can access anywhere
